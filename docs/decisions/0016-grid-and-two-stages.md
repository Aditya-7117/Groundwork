# 0016. The grid and the two-stage funnel

Date: 2026-09-24. Status: accepted.

## Context

Thirty setups (three chunkers, five first stages, reranker on or off) are cheap to score on
retrieval, but writing and judging an answer costs seconds of local generation and a paid call
each.

## Options

- Answer every question for every setup; or measure retrieval everywhere and answers for a few.
- Hand-written config files; or files generated from one definition.

## Decision

- Stage one: retrieval metrics for all 30 setups on all 3,220 questions.
- Stage two: answers and judgements for five setups (the BM25 baseline, the lowest-scoring setup
  as a weak reference, and the top three by passage nDCG@10) on 1,000 questions stratified by
  answer type with a fixed seed.
- The 30 configs are generated by `groundwork grid` from one definition and committed; a test
  fails if a committed file differs from what the generator writes.

## Consequences

- 1,000 questions give a standard error of about 1.6 points on a correctness rate, enough to
  separate setups five points apart.
- The weak reference spreads the judge-agreement study over good and bad answers.
